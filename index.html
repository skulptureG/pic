Pixel Timeline

Upload a picture. It’s sampled into a grid, then played left → right as a timeline that emits MIDI and OSC.

Tap or drop an image here

Browsers can’t send UDP, so OSC goes out as binary OSC packets over a WebSocket. Point it at a small WS→UDP bridge (or any WebSocket-capable OSC receiver). MIDI goes straight to any port, e.g. loopMIDI.
